Shyamsundar Holy Family School is a nurturing educational institution offering quality education from Play Group to Class 8. We believe every child is unique and deserves a personalised learning experience that helps them flourish.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive, and stimulating learning environment where every child develops academic excellence, strong values, and a love for lifelong learning.

🌟

Our Vision

To shape confident, compassionate, and creative young minds who contribute positively to society and are prepared to meet tomorrow's challenges.

Safety & Happiness

Your Child's Safety is Our Top Priority

Every measure, every policy, every decision we make starts with one question: Is this best for the child?

📷

CCTV Surveillance

Our entire campus is covered by CCTV cameras monitored by staff around the clock.

🚪

Controlled Entry/Exit

Only authorised parents and guardians can pick up students — verified at every gate.

🏥

First Aid Ready

Trained staff and a fully stocked first-aid room ensure prompt care for any situation.

🧹

Hygienic Campus

Daily sanitisation of classrooms, washrooms, and common areas keeps children healthy.

🤗

Anti-Bullying Policy

Zero tolerance for bullying. Our counsellors foster a kind, inclusive school culture.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Communication

Regular updates, parent-teacher meetings, and an open-door policy keep you informed.

😊
A Happy Child Learns Better

We celebrate every small win, encourage questions, and make every day at school something children look forward to.
